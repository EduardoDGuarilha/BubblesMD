- **Exact duplicates:** none among the 76 files, checked by SHA-256. This does not rule out the same content captured at another resolution or crop.
- **M01–M10:** one coherent homepage sequence. There is a capture-time gap between M06 and M07; completeness of the original webpage cannot be asserted. M08 includes both the fitness case and the start of the model-independent-context section.
- **B01–B11:** main Brain-page sequence. B12 and B13 were captured later as expanded figures from that topic. B10 embeds the lifecycle figure expanded in B13. B06 and B07 are adjacent text and its full diagram, not redundant text sources.
- **AB01–AB07:** coherent About-page sequence. Founder positions in AB04 are a snapshot, not a guarantee of current titles.
- **O01–O04 and O05–O08:** two capture sessions from the same Ontology topic. O01 begins at section 02; an earlier hero/section 01 is not supplied. O08 links to FG-O; the guide expands rather than duplicates the webpage.
- **H01–H13:** one topic sequence mixing page sections and expanded figures. H04/H05, H06/H07 and H08/H09 repeat diagram captions in short crops. H02 is an expanded harness figure. Those crops add readability, not independent evidence.
- **X01 and X02–X08:** two capture sessions from the same AI Spend Optimization topic. X03 expands the same routing diagram embedded in X04. The linked calculator itself is not supplied.
- **AE01–AE10:** page sections and expanded figures. AE02 embeds the pod figure expanded in AE03. AE04 is a cropped part of the build-loop figure shown in AE05. The depicted one operator, three engineers and eight weeks are illustration details, not a universal staffing or contract promise.
- **Cross-folder thematic repetition:** Brain governance B03/B04/B05/B06 overlaps the Harness/Governance page; B02 overlaps Spend Optimization’s catalog/routing discussion; B08/B09 overlap Agentic Engineering validation/delivery; M03/M04/M05 overlap the Brain and solution descriptions. These explain one foundation from different viewpoints.
- **Guide repetition:** all three reuse the mountain visual language, ownership thesis and closing message. FG-O discusses Palantir reference architecture; it does not prove Chiri implements the exact Palantir stack. FG-H and FG-G explain general disciplines as well as Chiri’s positioning.
- **Eduardo duplication:** MAG repeats its product and architecture in its closing summary and marketing narrative. CV overlaps MAG on stack and commercial context but is the stronger source for personal accomplishments. COL belongs to Rodrigo, not Eduardo.
