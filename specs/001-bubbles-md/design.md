# Bubbles.MD visual and interaction design

Status: design contract and historical references, not a verified app. This file owns visual requirements; implementation defaults can be adjusted for readability while preserving the selected experience.

Read/open `design/reference/left-layout-study.html`. It is a static design study with local demonstration controls, not application code or proof of editor behavior. The Paint concepts `design/reference/thought-bubble-concept.png` and `design/reference/minimized-bubble-concept.png`, if present, explain the bubble relationship. Do not copy their StackEdit branding or two-pane editor.

**Overrides to every reference:** only one writing representation is visible at a time; all minimized markers belong on the **right**; Enter submits reflections; the initial state has no Suggest button; Revise appears only after pops. The sketches illustrate relationships and interactions, not pixel templates, fixed coordinates or rectangular cards (revision of 2026-10-16). Study buttons that are disabled or illustrative must become real controls only where this contract specifies them. Do not copy fixed coordinates or the sketch's state logic into the app.

| Element | Implement this |
| --- | --- |
| Fresh opening | Bubbles.MD title, one pleasing create-document bubble, and one clickable file/drop area. No dashboard or onboarding wizard. Returning saved work restores the editor. |
| Canvas | Stable writing column toward the left, generous space on the right for reviews. Opening a review must not recenter or narrow the prose. |
| Desktop geometry | Starting defaults: 32–48 px outer space, 28–40 px document/review gap, readable prose around 58–68 characters per line. At roomy widths allow roughly 380–560 px for an expanded candidate; adapt to measured bounds. Values are starting points, not fixed screen coordinates. |
| Appearance | Quiet paper surface, dark readable text, restrained toolbar. Bubbles evoke soap bubbles and thought bubbles: translucent film, thin luminous rim, restrained iridescence, subtle volume and a small glint, with gentle floating and an inflate/settle entrance (paused under the pointer and with reduced motion). Text stays readable and controls easy to target. Green/yellow/red keep their edit meanings. System fonts, body ~16 px, 1.6–1.75 line height. |
| Bubble family | A compact connected family anchored near its passage. The reflections form one chain of thoughts (suggestion → first reflection → next → … → +) that flows left to right and wraps, with dots between consecutive bubbles taken from their actual edges.
| Collisions | Placement uses the measured bounds of the suggestion, reflections, controls, minimized reviews and connector dots together, including the animation envelope (float, inflate overshoot, hover scale). Bubbles never overlap; connector dots route around bubbles and minimized reviews or are omitted rather than drawn through them. Layout is recomputed when a bubble is created, expanded, resized, wraps or is removed, and on scroll and resize. Every bubble sizes to its content within viewport limits (a two-word reflection stays a small pill). Thought dots are computed from the actual passage and bubble positions and follow scrolling, resizing, wrapping and content changes; no static decorative dots or uniform full-width blocks. |
| Candidate contents | Only the replacement for the selection, including an incomplete sentence when that is what was selected. Do not repeat the original in another text block or silently expand the scope to a paragraph. |
| Source changes | Green insertion + at a position; yellow outlined/tinted original wording for an alteration; red outlined/tinted original wording for a deletion. Keep letters readable. No nested bubble outlines inside the candidate. |
| Candidate changes | Simple green/yellow emphasis on inserted/replacement text; removed text stays visible in the original. Hover or keyboard focus highlights the corresponding source and suggestion spans together (no connecting line), with Added/Altered/Deleted labels. Clicking either side pops the change. |
| Minimized state | Small translucent … bubbles on the right near their passages, grouped into a small cluster when several sit close together. Hide colored source-change decorations for minimized reviews. Markers stay distinguishable and clickable. |
| Plus controls | Every + is optically centred in its circle (a geometric icon, not a font glyph). The selection action sits beside the selection's last line; the add-reflection + stays with its family; insertion markers align with the text without changing line height. |
| Long/edge cases | Measure sizes and resolve collisions within review space. Bound a long candidate to available viewport height with reachable scrolling/actions; let attached thoughts remain reachable. Never cover the original with an oversized bubble. |
| Source offscreen | Preserve the review, stop drawing a misleading connector, and show a small Back to passage control. User activation scrolls to its actual target. Do not silently jump the document. |
| Narrow screens | Stack the active review after the writing surface when two columns no longer fit. Preserve explicit source navigation; do not squeeze prose into a tiny column. Desktop is the primary assessment view, but controls must remain usable. |
| Feedback | A short synthesized soap-bubble pop (no beep or chime) synchronized with a brief rupture: the film flashes outward and a few droplets scatter. One sound per action, including when a whole family pops. Outside-click minimization stays quiet. Include mute, reduced-motion handling, visible focus, and keyboard equivalents. |

Inspect the real application at desktop widths such as 1440 and 1024, plus a narrow width such as 390. Check a long multi-paragraph selection, three reflections, nearby minimized reviews, wrapping changes, resizing, scrolling, and the source leaving view. Screenshots must show real editor content and controls. The study is a reference, not visual acceptance of the future app.
